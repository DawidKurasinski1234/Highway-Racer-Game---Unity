If you want to use this sound pack with "Realistic Engine Sounds 2" unity asset,
import "RES2_Prefabs-Italian_V10.unitypackage" into your project to get prefabs made for Realistic Engine Sounds 2. Just make sure Realistic Engine Sounds 2 is already imported into your project.
After importing the above mentioned .unitypackage, the new engine sound prefabs will be added in 3 different quality levels into the following folder: RealisticEngineSound\Assets\Prefabs\Engine_Prefabs
Drag and drop one of the newly imported prefab into a vehicle physics compatibility prefab and you're ready to test these new sounds (Realistic Engine Sounds 2 comes with pre-made compatibility made for some vehicle physic controllers).

Get Realistic Engine Sounds 2 here: https://bit.ly/3aZ50Ag

Importing the unitypackage without Realistic Engine Sounds 2 already being in your project, will give some errors in the newly imported prefabs.
